# 📊 Eniac Discount Strategy Analysis
```
Python and Pandas business analysis case study investigating whether product discounts benefit Eniac.
```
This project is a Python and Pandas case study group project completed by [Sarah](https://github.com/sarahalkhaledova-datascientist), [Satish](https://github.com/satish-shrestha-4u) and [Julien](https://github.com/Julien-Dufresne).

The goal of the project is to analyse whether product discounts benefit Eniac, an e-commerce company specialising in Apple products and curated tech accessories.

## 🏢 Business Context

Eniac is debating whether discounts help or hurt the business.

The Marketing Team believes discounts can improve customer acquisition, satisfaction, retention, and overall business growth.

However, investors are concerned that aggressive discounts may reduce revenue and weaken Eniac’s premium brand positioning.

## 🎯 Business Question

Are discounts beneficial for Eniac?

## 🧹 Data Quality Context

Before answering the business questions, the dataset required substantial cleaning because it contained inconsistencies and possible data corruption issues.

The project therefore begins with data quality assessment and cleaning to ensure that the final dataset is reliable enough for business analysis.

## 📌 Project Purpose
The purpose of this repository is to demonstrate my ability to:
- Clean and validate real-world datasets
- Analyse business questions using Python and Pandas
- Identify patterns and relationships in e-commerce data
- Create meaningful data visualisations
- Translate analytical findings into business recommendations

## 💡 Key Insights
The analysis identified several important patterns:

- 💸 Discounting was common across many sales transactions.
- 📉 Higher discount levels did not show a clear relationship with increased revenue.
- 🛒 Mid-range products generated strong order volume, while higher-value products contributed more significantly to revenue.
- 🏷️ Discount performance varied across different product categories.

## 🎯 Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the project recommends:

- Use a more selective discount strategy instead of applying broad discounts across all products.
- Focus promotions on specific products, categories, or seasonal sales periods.
- Adjust discount decisions based on category-level performance.
- Limit aggressive discounts on high-value products to protect margins and premium brand positioning.

## 📊 Project Outcome

This project demonstrates how data cleaning, exploratory analysis, visualisation, and business reasoning can be combined to answer a real-world commercial question.

Rather than simply analysing discount percentages, the project evaluates their potential relationship with sales, revenue, product performance, and business positioning to support more informed discounting decisions.
